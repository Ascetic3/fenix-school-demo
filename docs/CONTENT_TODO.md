# Content to verify

Do not invent or silently publish uncertain school information.

Current items that require confirmation before production use include:

- final demo-week conditions and the remaining `demoWeekFields`;
- current staff names, roles, subjects and approved portraits used in Student-mode teacher cards;
- rights/approval for Student-mode demo photography;
- current admissions details if they change;
- current prices and fees before any production release;
- current documents/download files;
- current contact channels, social links and address details;
- any statistics, results, testimonials or claims that are not directly verified by the school;
- final replacement of explicitly marked illustrative/demo assets.

When factual content changes, update `src/content.js` or the relevant verified content source rather than scattering new hard-coded facts through components.
